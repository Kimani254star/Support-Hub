What I've built

Acacia Books — ERP suite

Acacia Books

The main hub tying every module together — company setup, users, and the shared data every other app below reads from.

Acacia CRM

Leads, contacts, and a sales pipeline for tracking deals from first contact to close.

Acacia Sales Hub

Quotations and sales orders, built to work hand in hand with CRM and Inventory.

Acacia Inventory

Stock control, movements, price lists, bundles, and shipments across one or more warehouses.

Acacia Expense

Operating costs, recurring costs, cost centres, approvals, and receipts.

Acacia Payroll

Employees, attendance, payslips, and statutory deductions, with reports ready for filing.

Acacia Project

Projects, milestones, tasks, timesheets, and budgets for teams delivering client work.

Standalone tools

BiasharaStart KE

Helps someone in Kenya work out what a business really costs to start, by business type and county, before they commit any money.

Business Finance Calculator
